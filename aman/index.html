Neighborhood

The quieter northern edge of South Beach

Set directly on Collins Avenue at the quieter northern edge of South Beach, Roney Palace places you beside the ocean, the Miami Beachwalk and some of the area's best-known luxury resorts. The property shares its oceanfront setting with 1 Hotel South Beach, with The Setai and W South Beach nearby, and offers multiple restaurants and bars on site, from lobby-level dining to a rooftop restaurant and bar that welcomes visitors on a reservation basis.

Restaurants, cafés, boutiques, wellness studios and everyday conveniences are within easy walking distance, while the neighborhood feels calmer and more residential than the nightlife-heavy heart of South Beach farther south. It is beachfront living with an excellent balance of dining, wellness and the Miami Beach lifestyle, without living in the middle of the late-night party district.
