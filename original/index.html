Neighborhood

The quieter northern edge of South Beach

Approximately 23rd Street

Set directly on Collins Avenue at the quieter northern edge of South Beach, Roney Palace places you beside the ocean, the Miami Beachwalk and some of the area's best-known luxury resorts. The property shares its oceanfront setting with 1 Hotel South Beach, with The Setai and W South Beach nearby, and offers multiple restaurants and bars on site, from lobby-level dining to a rooftop restaurant and bar that welcomes visitors on a reservation basis.

Restaurants, cafés, boutiques, wellness studios and everyday conveniences are within easy walking distance, while the neighborhood feels calmer and more residential than the nightlife-heavy heart of South Beach farther south. It is beachfront living with an excellent balance of dining, wellness and the Miami Beach lifestyle, without living in the middle of the late-night party district.

Renter FAQ

Before you ask

What is the minimum stay?

The minimum stay is 30 nights; stays up to 365 nights are possible.

How much is the monthly rate?

Rates are seasonal, per month: December to April $9,000-$10,000 · May to June $6,000 · July to August $5,000-$6,000 · September to October $4,000-$5,000 · November $6,000. The exact rate depends on your dates and length of stay.

When is the apartment available?

The apartment is available now, ongoing (no fixed end date).

What deposit and fees apply?

Security deposit from $1,000 ($1,500 with an approved dog; $2,000 for stays of 60 nights or more), confirmed at booking; Cleaning fee $200.

Are utilities and Wi-Fi included?

Internet/Wi-Fi, air conditioning, electricity and standard residential utilities are included, subject to normal residential use.

How many guests does it sleep?

It sleeps up to 4: permanent queen-size bed in the bedroom, a queen-size air mattress for up to two guests, and a sofa for one.

Is there a full kitchen?

Yes, there is a full kitchen, with a dishwasher and a coffee maker.

Can I work remotely from the apartment?

There is a workspace for remote work, and Wi-Fi is included.

Is there parking?

Parking is available in the building.

What is the pet policy?

No cats; small dogs considered case by case with owner approval, subject to building rules; a pet deposit or fee may apply. With an approved dog the security deposit is $1,500.

What are the house rules?

No smoking; building quiet-hour rules apply; no parties or events.

How do I request dates?

Send me your move-in date, length of stay and number of guests; once we agree on dates and terms I hold the dates and confirm the deposit; I'm happy to do a short video walkthrough or a video call first. Best contact: oceanlivingm@gmail.com (I usually reply within the hour, 8am-10pm ET).
